Information Required to Apply for a Waiver
Students waiving out of the mandatory student health insurance plan must provide complete policy information (Insurance company name, address, website and phone number; policy holder’s name and date of birth; policy number; group number and group name if applicable; policy dates of coverage, as well as information on the benefits provided by your plan) on the waiver form. You will not be able to complete a waiver request without this information.

Waivers are valid for one semester only!

Apply for the Mandatory Waiver

Apply for the mandatory waiver at:  https://studentcenter.uhcsr.com/.
Type “Georgia State University” into "Let's start with your school's name" and select our university from the dropdown list.
The Georgia State University Open Enrollment page is displayed. Scroll down towards the bottom of the page and click on Waiver Coverage.
Enter your Date of Birth and Student ID. Press Next.
“Georgia State University Waiver Form” page is displayed. Please note that your health insurance must meet the minimum coverage requirements mandated by the University System of Georgia to be approved.
Complete the PERSONAL INFORMATION section.
Complete the WAIVER QUESTIONS section.
Complete the INSURANCE INFORMATION section.
Complete verification statement by signing and dating form.
Press Submit.
